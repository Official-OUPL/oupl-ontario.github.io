Strongest School is decided on a single meet. Each school's best five lifters by DOTS have their scores added together, and the highest team total wins. Classic (raw) only. A red mark beside a lifter's rank means their score counted toward their school's total.

Weight classes are assigned from bodyweight at weigh-in. Club logos are the property of their respective clubs.
